# A 3–5 minute demonstration

Keep the local app open at http://127.0.0.1:5173. Clearly introduce this as a synthetic Lahore municipal demonstration.

1. **Citizen report (45 seconds).** Choose Citizen. Select New report. Use Take photo on a supported phone or choose an existing photograph. Describe a blocked drain near MM Alam Road, choose a map point in Gulberg and submit. Show the report reference, saved photograph and analysis provenance. Explain whether an actual model analyzed the image or the transparent text fallback ran.
2. **Intelligence (45 seconds).** Sign out and choose Authority. Find the new reference in Incidents. Show the classification and reviewable duplicate candidates. Open Disaster intelligence, select the clearly labeled heavy rainfall scenario and show the changed risk, factors, timestamp and synthetic source. Explain that the numerical engine supplies the score.
3. **Coordination (30 seconds).** Open Agent activity. Show the three specialist outputs and shared context. Approve a recommendation. Open the report, choose Response and assign the sanitation team with instructions. Approval and actual assignment are separate human decisions.
4. **Worker evidence (45 seconds).** Sign out and choose Worker. Open the assigned incident, accept it and start work. Upload a completion photograph, describe cleared drainage and submit. The persisted status becomes Resolved, awaiting verification.
5. **Close the loop (30 seconds).** Choose Authority, inspect completion evidence and update to Closed with a verification reason. Choose Citizen, reopen the report and show the final status and notification.
6. **CRUD and traceability (30 seconds).** Choose Admin and create/edit/deactivate a test department or zone. Reload to demonstrate persistence. Show incident activity, or demonstrate a reversible withdrawal/archive. For duplicate demonstrations, submit two similarly described reports within roughly 180 metres during the same week, review the match, confirm and then separate it.

An open manhole or electrical cable follows the same civic workflow without being counted as a flood-related hazard. A heavy rainfall scenario changes risk independently of new submissions. The missing-weather scenario demonstrates uncertainty handling.

If live weather is unavailable, open provenance and show the recorded failure; use the explicitly synthetic scenario for repeatable scoring. Never call the seed records live city evidence. This application coordinates simulated work and does not dispatch emergency responders.

# Role navigation and retained capabilities

| Destination or action | Citizen | Authority | Worker | Admin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overview | Your civic dashboard | Command centre | Field workspace | Command centre |
| Reports and incident records | My reports | Incidents | My assignments | Incidents |
| Disaster intelligence | — | Available | — | Available |
| Agent activity and recommendation reviews | — | Available | — | Available |
| Administration | — | — | — | Available |
| Notifications | Available | Available | Available | Available |
| New report, photograph and Take photo | Available | — | — | — |
| Assignment and authority verification | — | Available | — | Available |
| Worker progress and completion evidence | — | — | Available | — |

Administration retains Departments, Teams, Zones, Users, and Audit history. Organizational records support create, inspect, edit, deactivate and reactivate through the existing authenticated API. Report creation belongs to the Citizen workflow; report editing follows the stored status/ownership rules. These permissions are enforced by the backend as well as navigation.

To inspect every management destination in the local demonstration, sign in using the **Admin** button. To submit a photograph/location report, use **Citizen**. The visible navigation reflects the active role shown in the sidebar profile.
